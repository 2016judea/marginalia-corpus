Use the Marginalia Corpus in Claude

A read-only MCP server over the marks famous readers left in their books. Ask a question in plain words; Claude calls the tools and quotes the source.

Server URL

https://marginalia-corpus.vercel.app/mcp

Streamable HTTP. No account, no key, no sign-in. Every tool is read-only.

Add it

  1. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors.
  2. Choose Add custom connector.
  3. Name it Marginalia Corpus, paste the URL above, and save. No authentication.
  4. In a chat, turn the connector on from the tools menu and ask.

Try

  • “What did Melville mark in Shakespeare?”
  • “Who read Homer, and what kind of evidence is there?”
  • “Which authors did both Melville and Beckett read?”

Tools

  • reader_marksWhat a reader marked in an author or a book“What did Melville mark in Shakespeare?”
  • who_read_authorWho read an author: marked, held or borrowed, or named“Who read Homer, and what is the evidence?”
  • reader_profileA reader's profile: volumes, most-marked authors, dates“Tell me about John Dee's library.”
  • authors_read_by_manyAuthors reached by more than one reader“Which authors did both Melville and Beckett read?”
  • search_marginaliaSearch the quotable marginalia and marked passages“Did Melville mark “Nothing can come of nothing”?”
  • about_the_corpusWhat is in the Marginalia Corpus, its sources and terms“Where does this data come from?”

What comes back

Counts of each kind of evidence, kept separate; the volumes, with edition, library and shelfmark; and, where the archive allows quotation, the passage itself with the citation it asks for. For the Archaeology of Reading, the Walt Whitman Archive and the Beckett Digital Library the connector returns counts and metadata only: their terms do not permit republishing the transcriptions. No archive images are ever served or linked.
