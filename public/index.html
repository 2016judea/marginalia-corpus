Three kinds of evidence, never added together

Own words: the reader wrote in the margin. Bare marks: a score, underline or checkmark beside a passage. Attested copies: the reader owned or borrowed the book and no mark survives. A reader naming an author in their own prose is a fourth thing, a reference, and is counted apart.
